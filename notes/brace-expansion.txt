Brace Expansion in Bash
This is a shortcut for generating multiple words, filenames, or paths without typing them all out.

Instead of writing:
file1.txt file2.txt file3.txt

Write:
file{1,2,3}.txt

To create multiple files:
touch report{1,2,3}.txt
``
Making Directories:
mkdir -p project/{src,bin,docs,tests}

Creating numbered pages: (Use "..")
mkdir project{1..5}

Combining braces: (Use "..")
echo {dev,test,prod}-{01..03} gives

dev-01 dev-02 dev-03
test-01 test-02 test-03
prod-01 prod-02 prod-03

Wildcard expansion
echo *.txt - shows .txt files that actually exist.
